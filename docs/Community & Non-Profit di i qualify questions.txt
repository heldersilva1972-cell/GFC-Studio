Community & Non-Profit Rental Discount Application
This program provides reduced hall rental rates to registered non-profits, organized civic and youth groups, and grassroots charitable fundraisers.

To qualify, an applicant must answer YES to at least one category in Section 1, and YES to all criteria in Section 2.

Section 1: Qualifying Entity or Cause (Check all that apply)
Answering YES to at least one category satisfies the organizational criteria:

[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — Registered Non-Profit / Civic Entity

The booking is made on behalf of an IRS-recognized tax-exempt organization (501(c)(3), 501(c)(4), 501(c)(7), 501(c)(19), etc.).

Attach: IRS Determination Letter or state non-profit certificate.

[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — School or Youth Program

The event represents an accredited school, PTO/PTA, scouts troop, or non-profit youth sports organization.

Attach: Letter on official school or league letterhead.

[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — Local Civic or Community Group

The booking is for an official meeting, assembly, or function of a recognized neighborhood council, civic association, or volunteer service club.

Attach: Officer contact information and meeting announcement/flyer.

[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — Charitable Benefit / Relief Drive

The event is a direct fundraiser or supply drive for a specific individual, family, or disaster relief effort (e.g., medical fundraiser, memorial benefit).

Attach: Summary of the beneficiary, cause, and fund distribution method.

Section 2: Event Integrity & Terms (All must be YES)
[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — Dedicated Purpose: The hall will be used solely for an official meeting, community program, or charitable fundraiser, and not for a personal social celebration (e.g., birthday, wedding, anniversary, graduation).

[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — No Private Profit: 100% of event proceeds (ticket sales, donations, raffles) will go directly to the named organization or beneficiary, with zero personal or commercial profit.

[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — Organizational Payment: Contract execution and rental payments will be issued directly from the organization's account or the dedicated fundraiser fund.

[ ] YES  |  [ ] NO — Standard Deposit Terms: The applicant acknowledges that the discount applies solely to the base hall rental rate; standard security deposits, cleaning fees, and rental rules remain fully payable and enforceable.

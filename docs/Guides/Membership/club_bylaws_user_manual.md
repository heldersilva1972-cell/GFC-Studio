# GFC Web Application: Club Bylaws User Manual & Standard Operating Procedure

---

## Document Control & Purpose
* **Module:** Governance & Legal Framework
* **Primary URL:** `/bylaws`
* **Target Audience:** Club Officers, Board of Directors, Members, Legal / Parliamentarian Committee
* **Purpose:** This manual provides an exhaustive, non-technical guide on accessing, browsing, searching, and managing the official **GFC Club Constitution & Bylaws**. It covers keyword searching for parliamentary rules, printing complete or section-specific bylaws rulebooks, and recording ratified amendments with official meeting references.

---

## 1. How to Navigate to the Club Bylaws Page

Follow these exact steps from anywhere inside the web application:

1. **Locate the Left Navigation Sidebar:**
   Find the dark navigation sidebar along the left edge of your screen.
2. **Expand the Operations Section:**
   Under **OPERATIONS**, click on **MEMBERSHIP** to expand its sub-menu.
3. **Click "Club Bylaws":**
   Click on the item labeled **Club Bylaws** (accompanied by the document icon <i class="bi bi-file-earmark-text"></i>).
4. **Confirmation:**
   The page loads with the header **GFC Constitution & Bylaws** and displays the interactive article navigator and reader.

## 3. Step-by-Step Operating Procedures

### Procedure A: Browsing and Reading Articles
1. In the **Table of Contents** on the left side of the screen, click on any Article title (for example, **Article IV: Meetings & Elections**).
2. The right-hand pane instantly scrolls to that article.
3. Sub-sections and clauses are clearly numbered and organized for quick reading during board meetings.

---

### Procedure B: Searching for Specific Rules During a Meeting
If a question arises during a meeting regarding meeting quorum, nomination deadlines, or rental discounts:

1. Click into the **Search Bylaws** box located at the top right of the page.
2. Type a keyword (for example, *"quorum"*, *"nomination"*, *"life"*, or *"expulsion"*).
3. Press **Enter** or pause for a moment:
   * The system immediately searches the entire document.
   * Matching sections appear in the search results with the search term **highlighted in bright yellow**.
4. Click on any search snippet to jump straight to that exact paragraph in the bylaws reader.

### Procedure C: Printing or Generating an Official PDF Bylaws Booklet
1. In the upper right corner, click the **"Print / Export"** dropdown button (<i class="bi bi-printer"></i>).
2. Choose from the available options:
   * **Full Bylaws Rulebook (PDF):** Generates a complete, professionally formatted booklet with title page, table of contents, and all articles for distribution to new members.
   * **Current Article Only (PDF):** Prints only the specific article you are currently viewing.
3. Your browser will open the print preview or download the PDF file immediately.

### Procedure D: Recording an Official Amendment (Authorized Officers Only)
When an amendment to the bylaws has been properly submitted, read, and ratified by member vote:

1. Navigate to the Article and Section that was amended.
2. If logged in with administrative privileges, click the button labeled **"Edit Section"** (<i class="bi bi-pencil-square"></i>) located at the bottom of the section.
3. An amendment editor window will open:
   * **Section Text:** Edit the official wording to reflect the new ratified language.
   * **Ratification Meeting Date:** Enter the exact date of the meeting where the membership approved the change.
   * **Vote Tally / Certification:** Enter the certified vote (e.g., *"Passed 42-6 at Annual General Meeting on November 20, 2026. Certified by Recording Secretary."*).
4. Click **"Save Amendment"**.
5. **Immediate System Updates:**
   * The public text updates immediately.
   * An archival footnote is permanently added to the bottom of that section indicating the amendment date.
   * The "Last Amended" date on the main screen updates automatically.
   * An entry is written to the system Security Audit Log.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Who has permission to edit the bylaws in the system?
* Only users with an active **Admin** staff role or specifically granted permission to the Bylaws management tool can access the "Edit Section" button. Standard staff, bartenders, and regular members have read-only access.

### Q2: Can past wording of an article be retrieved if needed?
* Yes. Every time an amendment is saved, the previous wording is preserved in the system's audit history table, allowing the club parliamentarian or legal counsel to view historical versions from past years.
